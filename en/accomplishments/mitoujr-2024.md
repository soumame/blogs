## What is Mitou Junior

> A program that supports elementary, junior high, and high school students and [高専生](https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/koutou/kousen/index.htm) aged 17 or younger who have original ideas and outstanding technical skills.
> The program **Mitou Junior** was established and is run mainly by alumni of the [未踏事業](https://www.ipa.go.jp/jinzai/mitou/koubo/), a human resource development program for people aged 24 and under carried out by the Information-technology Promotion Agency ([IPA](https://www.ipa.go.jp/)), an independent administrative agency under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry.
> Mitou Junior began in 2016, and to date has received **860 applications and supported/selected 136 creators**. - [未踏ジュニアとは - 未踏ジュニア](https://jr.mitou.org/about)
