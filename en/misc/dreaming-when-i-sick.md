The moment I woke from the dream, I still remembered it, and it seems I made a voice memo to record what I remembered, even though I was groggy. When I listened back later I thought the text was so strange it felt creepy, but since it was something I wouldn't normally write, I'm putting it down here.

## Main text (verbatim
I went to the hospital and had a craniotomy.
I don't know what was wrong with me, but I went to the hospital, they opened my head, and performed surgery that used electrical stimulation to adjust my brain activity so it would become more favorable and superior.
I was told that if I imagined the flame as instructed, it would appear in my visual field, so I was told I just needed to keep doing that. The images were really oddly and beautifully connected, and I thought that was strange. However, after viewing about two of the images, an enormous sense of fatigue came over me. In the end I stopped partway through. Is that okay?
