When I woke from the dream, I still had the memory, and while still groggy it seems I made a voice memo to record what I remembered. When I later looked back at it, the text was so strange it felt gross, but since it was something I wouldn't normally write, I'm recording it here.

## Main Text (Original as-is)
I went to the hospital and had a craniotomy
I don't know what was wrong with me, but at the hospital they opened my head and performed a procedure where they used electrical stimulation — applying electricity there to adjust my brain activity so that it would become more favorable and superior.
They told me that if I imagined the flame exactly as instructed, it would appear in my visual field, so I was told I just needed to keep doing that. The images were really strangely and beautifully connected and I thought it was odd. However, after watching about two of the images, an overwhelming fatigue came over me. In the end I stopped halfway through. Is that okay?
