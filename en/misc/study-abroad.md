Lately, without any particular reason, I’ve been thinking that I want to go abroad.

When I really think about it, I started wondering why I want to go.

Originally, I had spent about five years abroad, and then I came back.

Now I’m attending a university in Japan, and I’ve been thinking I want to go abroad again—an exchange program would be fine too.

And when I rethink my motives, there isn’t really any deep reason.

Like, going abroad looks cool? I don’t really know the reason. When I skim things online, people who’ve been abroad often seem successful. Since I was actually able to change a lot myself, I wonder if it has a positive influence. Also, because I can speak English to some extent, I started wondering what it even means to stay in Japan.

But if I think about it carefully, I don’t really know what it means to be in Japan either; you could also ask what it means to go abroad — so I’m not sure.

On top of that, foreign universities are supposed to be amazing. You can learn cutting-edge stuff. Some people say Japanese education is finished/behind the times, but when I actually look into it, it doesn’t feel that way at all. It’s just full of things I don’t know.

I don’t know.
