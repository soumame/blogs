Lately, I haven't really had a particular reason, but I've been feeling like I want to go abroad.

When I actually thought about it again, I started wondering why I want to go.

Originally, I had spent about five years abroad and then came back. Now I'm attending a university in Japan, and I've been thinking I'd like to go abroad again—even as an exchange student.

And when I reconsidered my motives, they weren't really that deep.

Like, is it just that going abroad looks cool? I don't really know the reason. When I skim things on the internet, it seems like people who go abroad are successful. I myself was able to change a lot, so I wonder if it would have a positive effect. Also, since I can speak English to some extent, I've started questioning what the point is of staying in Japan.

But if I think about it, I don't really know the meaning of being in Japan either. You could also ask what the point of going abroad is. I just don't really know.

Also, when people talk about overseas universities, they say they're amazing—you can learn cutting-edge stuff. Some people say Japanese education is finished, but when I actually look into it, it doesn't feel that way at all. There's so much I don't know.

I don't know what to make of it.
