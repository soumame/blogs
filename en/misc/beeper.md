Beeper is just genuinely useful.

https://www.beeper.com/

Lately I've been communicating with a lot of people, and messages are often split across different apps, so it's not uncommon to miss messages. Having a native app is already better, but some services like Messenger are web-only and rely solely on browser notifications, which makes the experience much worse compared to a smartphone.

Using Beeper lets you consolidate messages from multiple platforms into a nicely designed UI app, so I've found it extremely handy.

And today (6/23), the official announcement added LINE support, so finally my messaging is coming together in Beeper

https://blog.beeper.com/2026/06/23/beeper-june/

### Data storage
To be honest, given the nature of the app, it feels inevitable to entrust data to Beeper, but privacy is still a concern, so I looked into it.
#### Stored in the cloud
This offers the better experience, with smooth syncing across devices. However, you should be aware that your data will be placed on servers provided by Beeper and viewed from there. (That said, once you're having conversations on Instagram, I personally think Meta already has your data, so I use the cloud.)
#### Local option
If you want to manage your data yourself or don't want it stored in the cloud, some apps offer an option to save data locally. There's no device-to-device sync, but in terms of where the data lives, this may feel safer.

### Pricing
Beeper is free to use at a basic level, but there are paid subscriptions. You can connect up to three accounts (apps) for free, which is probably enough in most cases (for example, Instagram + LINE + Messenger). The Plus subscription allows 10 accounts, and PlusPlus is unlimited. There are also finer differences in features, so check the details.
