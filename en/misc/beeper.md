Beeper is genuinely handy.

https://www.beeper.com/

Lately I've been interacting with a lot of people, and messages are often split across different apps, so it's not uncommon to miss something. Having a native app helps, but some services like Messenger are only available as web apps, relying solely on browser notifications, and the experience is noticeably worse compared to a smartphone.

With Beeper, you can consolidate messages from multiple platforms into a single app with a nice UI, so I've found it really useful.

And today (6/23) they announced official LINE support, so finally my messaging is going to be consolidated in Beeper.

https://blog.beeper.com/2026/06/23/beeper-june/

### Data storage methods
Frankly, given the nature of the app, entrusting data to Beeper feels inevitable, but I was still curious about privacy so I looked into it.

#### Cloud storage
This is the superior experience in terms of usability — it syncs smoothly across devices. However, you should be aware that your data will be stored on servers provided by Beeper and viewed from there. (That said, once you're chatting on platforms like Instagram, I assume Meta already has your data, so using the cloud is fine for me.)

#### Local support
If you prefer to manage your data yourself or don't want it in the cloud, some clients offer an option to store data locally. You won't get device-to-device sync, but it can feel safer in terms of where your data lives.

### Pricing
The basic service is free, but there are paid subscriptions. You can connect up to three accounts (apps) for free, which will probably be enough for most people (for example: Instagram + LINE + Messenger).

The Plus subscription allows 10 accounts, and PlusPlus provides unlimited accounts. There are other feature differences worth checking.
